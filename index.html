Why Hyaecord?

Plugin ecosystem compatibility

Runs most Equicord and Vencord plugins through a dedicated compatibility layer, with Equicord Cloud Saves for settings sync.

Native look & feel

Designed around GTK4/Libadwaita and KDE Plasma conventions — auto theme matching on GNOME and KDE, with WCAG AA Light, Dark, and AMOLED themes built in.

Event-driven performance

No background polling loops. Gaming Mode keeps resource use minimal during play while mentions, DMs, calls, and push-to-talk keep working.

Accessibility first

Screen reader semantics, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, independent text/UI scaling, and non-color-dependent notification cues.

Frictionless setup

Flatpak, AppImage, deb, rpm, and AUR packages — no terminal required. A first-run wizard imports your existing Discord, Vesktop, or Equibop login.

Built-in integrations

UserPFP, UsrBG, GlobalBadges, and RPC Bridge ship as first-class features, not plugins you have to hunt down.
