Everything you'd want. Nothing you wouldn't.

An original client — not a reskin — engineered around how your desktop actually works.

🔌

A real plugin API

Sandboxed, ergonomically modeled on Vencord's plugin shape — simple message-hook plugins port easily. Not byte-compatible with existing plugin files yet; see the Plugin Guidelines for the honest scope.

🖥️

Feels native

Designed around GTK4/Libadwaita and KDE Plasma. Auto-matches your GNOME or KDE theme on first launch.

⚡

Event-driven speed

No polling loops burning your CPU. Fast startup, low idle usage, and GPU-accelerated motion that respects reduced-motion.

🎮

Gaming Mode

Event-driven fullscreen detection (X11/XWayland) keeps mentions and DMs coming through while you play, without a background polling loop burning CPU.

♿

Accessible by default

Screen reader semantics, full keyboard navigation, independent text/UI scaling, and WCAG AA Light & Dark themes, including every community theme in the Theme Store.

🎁

Batteries included

UserPFP, UsrBG, GlobalBadges, and RPC Bridge are all live — real community avatar/banner databases and a real local Rich Presence socket for external apps. Every one optional and off-switchable in Settings.

🛡️

Private by design

One anonymous daily ping (random ID, version, OS) with a single opt-out — never your messages, account, or behaviour. Everything else that transmits data is opt-in.

🧩

Fair plugin rules

No paywalls, ever. Creators get donation links, users get free plugins, and abusive tooling is permanently banned.

🦴

Real Discord, today

Messaging, member lists, server folders, real GIF search, profile popouts, and server banners already work. Voice, calls, and multi-account are still on the roadmap — tracked openly in the repo, not hidden.

🦡

Stoat.chat too

Real, independent Stoat.chat support alongside Discord — its own REST/gateway integration, not a lookalike. Log into either or both and merge them into one server rail, badged per platform. Fluxer is a planned next step.

Wait, how do you even say "Hyaecord"?

The casual answer: HY-eh-kord, rhymes with "Discord." The pedantic answer follows, entirely unprompted.

/haɪˈiːnɪˌdiː kɔːrdɪs/

Hyaecord is, taxonomically speaking, a portmanteau of Hyaenidae — the Latin family name for hyenas, itself borrowed from the Ancient Greek ὕαινα (hýaina) — and chorda, Latin for "cord" or "string" (via the Greek χορδή). Naturally, the only correct way to say it is to decline both halves in full Classical Latin, as though reciting a genitive noun phrase from a Linnaean species description.

Hy-as in Hyaenidae, nominative plural: "of the hyena-kind"
-ae-the Latin diphthong æ — /aɪ/ if you're Reconstructed Classical, /iː/ if you're Ecclesiastical, and both if you're insufferable
-cor-from chorda, genitive chordis: "of the string"
-disgenitive singular suffix, because apparently the chord now belongs to the hyena family

Said correctly, in full Reconstructed Classical Latin, with appropriate reverence for a fictional taxonomic ancestor: /hyːˈae̯.nɪ.dae̯ ˈkʰor.d̪ɪs/ — roughly "hew-EYE-nih-die KOR-diss," delivered as though defending a doctoral thesis on Miocene carnivorans.

Everyone at Hyaecord just says "HY-eh-kord." You are welcome to do the same.
