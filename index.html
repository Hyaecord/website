Everything you'd want. Nothing you wouldn't.

An original client — not a reskin — engineered around how your desktop actually works.

🔌

Your plugins, unchanged

A compatibility layer runs most Equicord and Vencord plugins out of the box, with Equicord Cloud Saves for settings sync.

🖥️

Feels native

Designed around GTK4/Libadwaita and KDE Plasma. Auto-matches your GNOME or KDE theme on first launch.

⚡

Event-driven speed

No polling loops burning your CPU. Fast startup, low idle usage, and GPU-accelerated motion that respects reduced-motion.

🎮

Gaming Mode

Focus-aware across monitors: resource use drops while you play, but mentions, DMs, calls, and push-to-talk keep working.

♿

Accessible by default

Screen reader semantics, full keyboard navigation, independent text/UI scaling, and WCAG AA Light, Dark & AMOLED themes.

🎁

Batteries included

UserPFP, UsrBG, GlobalBadges, and RPC Bridge are built in — every one optional and off-switchable in Settings.

🛡️

Private by design

One anonymous daily ping (random ID, version, OS) with a single opt-out — never your messages, account, or behaviour. Everything else that transmits data is opt-in.

🧩

Fair plugin rules

No paywalls, ever. Creators get donation links, users get free plugins, and abusive tooling is permanently banned.

🦴

Nothing lost

Calls, screen share, search, pins, IME input, emoji/GIF pickers, multi-account, keybinds — full parity with stock Discord.
